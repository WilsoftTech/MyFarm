## Policies proposed at the planning checkpoint

The following proposals were subsequently approved by the owner on 2026-10-29 under D-P05-002 and D-P05-003; this section preserves what was presented:

1. Quantity is an exact decimal string with up to 12 whole digits and 6 fractional places. Crop harvests initially use kilograms; birds and eggs use separate whole-count units. No implicit bag conversions. Reject movements that make stock negative.
2. A harvest references a named same-farm storage location, has optional free-text grade, and records loss separately from gross harvested quantity. Inventory monetary valuation remains unavailable until an approved Phase 8 costing policy.
3. A stock-linked sale atomically creates one stock effect and one Phase 4 sale. A bird sale decrements the flock once. Existing accounting-only sales retain their meaning and do not automatically acquire stock effects.

The first two decisions affect T001. The third must be resolved before sale/flock posting tasks. Storage creation, late harvests against closed cycles, correction behavior and sale-link/reversal details must be specified explicitly in the affected contract before persistence; the plan does not silently approve them.
