## Changes

`src/components/analytics/trends.tsx` adds a "Compared with before" section under the existing figures. Each crop card says which seasons it compares ("2027A compared with 2026B"); each batch card names the batch before it. Every line shows the earlier figure, the current one and the change ("Change: +18%"), per currency, with one line per cost type. A missing figure or baseline shows its plain reason, never 0. Costs that may belong to a side but are not linked to it are named, with a warning that the comparison may mislead. A side still open is labelled "so far".

The screen states that it shows how figures moved, not why. For all records or chosen dates it asks for a season before comparing crops, while batches still show. Every figure comes from the service; the component calculates nothing. The arrow between figures is hidden from screen readers, which hear "then" instead.
