**Who this is for:** the project owner and reviewers of Phase 11.

## What T004 adds

**One evidence shape for every tool** (`domain/evidence.ts`):
- **Fields:** every tool returns its farm, period, how money was counted, the time it was calculated, a currency, figures (money, count, percent or date, each an exact string) and labels (enterprise, category, record kind, activity, season, alert, subject).
- **Outcome:** either the refs the answer must cite, or a stated reason there is no conclusion.
- **One set of checks and one display** serve every tool: the T001 checks and the T003 screen.
- **What a provider sees:** labels go out as their ref and kind only, never the farmer's words (D-P11-003).
- **Highest profit** moved to this shape; its answers are unchanged.

**The seven question types from the source (P0627-P0633),** each bounded, read-only, at version 1, and naming what it reads (`domain/tools.ts`):

| Question type | Reads | Bound | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ask records | Money records, latest first (Phase 4) | 8 records; reversed ones left out | Date, kind, category, amount and enterprise of each, each in its own currency |
| Explain profit | Farm screen (Phase 8) | One answer per currency | Earned, spent, recorded surplus, margin; a loss said as an INFERENCE; "not your full profit" |
| Compare seasons | Farm screen for the chosen season and the one that started just before it | Per currency | Both surpluses and the change, earned and spent in each; needs one season chosen; a currency in only one season is not compared |
| Summarize | Farm screen | Per currency | Surplus, top enterprise, how many enterprises are below zero |
| Unusual costs | Alerts: the five approved cost rules (Phase 10) | 5 alerts | Each open cost alert with its figures. "Unusual" means only what the owner already approved for alerts; no new threshold |
| Upcoming tasks | Activities due and missed (Phase 6) | 6 tasks | Work due from today and work past its date |
| Farm report | Farm screen with costs by kind | 5 enterprises | Totals, labour, inputs and other costs, enterprise margins; says the assistant makes no file and points to the existing downloads |

Every plain answer stays within 12 statements and says when a list is not complete.

## Tests

`tests/unit/ai-assistant-tools.test.ts`, 11 tests; `ai-assistant-domain.test.ts` updated to the new shape.

- **Golden answers for each type,** as the farmer reads them: for example "The recorded surplus was UGX 720000 in 2026A and UGX 500000 in 2025B: a change of UGX 220000."
- **Every plain answer** passes the same checks an explainer's must.
- **Stale context:** later records give new evidence with its own calculation time, and an earlier answer is never rewritten.
- **Refused exports:** the report makes no file, contains no link or path, and points to the farm's own downloads, which keep their limits and audit.
- **Provider outage:** for every tool, the fake's answer built from the explain request alone equals the plain answer shown on outage.
- **Units, currencies, dates, scope:** money always carries its own currency and is never combined; dates are figures; each evidence belongs only to its farm.
- **Privacy:** no label text, label ID, farm or tenant ID, or season name appears in any tool's explain request.
- The cost-rule titles match the Alerts page word for word.
