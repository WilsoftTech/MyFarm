## Screen behaviour

- **Plain words:**
  - The four record kinds appear as Expense, Other income, Sale and Purchase, each with guidance on which to choose. For example, money paid at the time is an expense, while something paid later is a purchase.
  - Amounts still owed appear as "Still to receive" or "Still to pay" with "Received/Paid so far", not as receivable/payable.
  - The sale detail states that payments reduce what is due and do not count as another sale.
- **Entry:**
  - Each form shows a category list filtered by kind, an amount (digits only, no commas), a currency (default UGX), a date and an optional buyer/supplier/paid-to name.
  - Only expense and other income ask how the money was paid or received. Sale and purchase forms have no such field, and the strict schema would reject one.
  - Invalid money, an unknown currency or a missing category is announced next to the control, and focus moves to the first invalid field.
- **Payments:**
  - "Record money received" (sale) or "Record a payment" (purchase) opens a form prefilled with what is still due. The farmer can enter part of it.
  - The server derives the direction, currency and the single allocation from the authorized record, so the client cannot choose them.
  - Overpayment returns a clear message, and the draft is kept.
- **Reversal:**
  - A reason and an explicit "I understand that reversing is final" confirmation are required.
  - A sale or purchase that has recorded payments shows "first reverse each payment" instead of the form. Payment pages link back to the record they settle.
  - Reversed records stay listed with their reason and original amount.
- **States:** empty list, end of list, "nothing still due", "reversed", saved/paid/reversed notices, and loading status.
  - Offline: forms disable saving and keep the draft, and reconnecting saves once.
  - Lost response: the retry reuses the original request ID and records exactly one payment.
  - Revoked membership: the form shows "no access" and keeps the draft.
  - Expired session: the page redirects to sign-in.
  - Another farm, another user or an admin: the page redirects to `/forbidden`.
  - An unknown record kind in the URL renders the not-found page.
- **Mobile:** single column, no horizontal scrolling at Pixel 7 width. Screenshots are under `.cache/p04-t003-visual/`.

Plot, enterprise and season links on records are not on these screens; they belong to R003 (T007–T009). Totals, categories summaries and gross margin are R004/R005.
