## What T003 adds

**A new page, "Ask about this farm"** (`/farms/{farmId}/assistant`), reached by a new "Ask" link in the farm's navigation:

- **Question box.** It holds up to 500 letters. Its hint says not to type names or phone numbers, because the question is kept with the answer (D-P11-006). A "Which records" choice offers a season or all records, starting on the farm screen's own default: the latest active season, or all records. The season is never read from the question.
- **Answers.** Each statement carries its label: *Fact from your records*, *What this suggests*, *Suggestion* or *Not certain*. Every figure, enterprise name and period comes from MyFarm's evidence, and amounts are grouped for reading (UGX 1,420,000). Under each answer:
  - where it came from: the period, how money is counted, and when it was calculated, with "ask again for the latest figures";
  - who worded it: "Worded by MyFarm" or "Worded by the assistant and checked by MyFarm".
- **Your questions.** Newest first. The page says only the asker can see them and each is kept 90 days. Each shows its delete date and a "Delete this question" button.
- **Errors and offline.** Offline, the form keeps the question and won't send. An empty question is stopped before sending. Over the daily limit, the message says 20 questions have been asked in the last 24 hours, not "wait a minute". To make that possible, `runServerCommand` takes an optional limit message; the per-minute form-save limit keeps its own wording.
