## Rules

- **Money (D-P04-002):** amounts are positive decimal strings with up to 16 whole digits and 4 decimals, matching numeric(20,4). Forms may send digit strings, where leading zeros and trailing fraction zeros normalize (`0150000.50` → `150000.5`). APIs may also send safe integers. Signs, exponents, separators (`150,000`), fractional JSON numbers and blank values are rejected. Arithmetic uses BigInt units of 0.0001, so `0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3` exactly. A currency is any ISO 4217 code known to the runtime's `Intl` list (trimmed and uppercased). No code combines or converts different currencies. Tax is out of scope.
- **Record kinds (D-P04-003):** a SALE or PURCHASE is the single revenue or cost event and opens a receivable or payable. INCOME and EXPENSE are for items that are not sales or purchases, and are settled when recorded, so only they may name a payment method. A sale or purchase carrying a payment method is rejected. Sales and income use REVENUE categories; purchases and expenses use COST categories. A wrong-kind category is INVALID_INPUT, and an archived category or payment method is CONFLICT for new records.
- **Links:** plot, enterprise and season are optional, and the farm always owns the record. Each supplied link must match a parent looked up under the authorized farm; another farm's parent or a substituted ID is FORBIDDEN. Closed enterprises and seasons remain linkable, so a late bill keeps its allocation. `activityId` is rejected until Phase 6. Deeper plot/enterprise/season consistency belongs to R003 (T007).
- **Receivable/payable:** always derived as obligation total minus POSTED allocations, and never stored as authority. The states are OPEN, PARTIALLY_SETTLED, SETTLED, or VOID once reversed. Corrupt history (over-settled, or allocations on a reversed obligation) is CONFLICT rather than a reported balance.
- **Payments:** a payment is RECEIVED (settles a sale) or MADE (settles a purchase), with a required payment method and an optional external reference. It must be fully allocated across 1–50 distinct obligations, with amounts that add up exactly. Advances and unapplied credit are not part of Phase 4. Each allocation must:
  - be in the payment's currency
  - be dated on or after the obligation
  - target a POSTED obligation
  - not exceed what is outstanding (CONFLICT)
- **Reversal (D-P04-002):** posted records are never edited. A reversal names the record type and ID and a reason of 1–500 characters, and happens at most once. A sale or purchase with POSTED allocations is reversed only after those payments are reversed. Reversing a payment reverses its allocations.
- **Recognition (D-P04-002/003):** ACCRUAL is the default basis. Every event carries its basis label.
  - **ACCRUAL:** each POSTED record is exactly one event on its own date, and payments add nothing.
  - **CASH:** income and expenses fall on their own date; sales and purchases count once per POSTED allocation, on the payment date.
  - **Both:** reversed records and payments are excluded, events are ordered deterministically, and incomplete or inconsistent source sets are rejected.
  - **Not in T001:** totals, gross margin and reports belong to R004/R005.

AC001 domain oracle: a UGX 200,000 sale with UGX 80,000 received is PARTIALLY_SETTLED with 120,000 outstanding. Accrual recognizes one 200,000 revenue event on the sale date; cash recognizes one 80,000 event on the payment date. Neither basis counts both. A further 120,000 settles the sale exactly, and 120,000.0001 is a CONFLICT.

## Interpretations to confirm

- INCOME and EXPENSE are settled when recorded; anything on credit is recorded as a SALE or PURCHASE. This follows from D-P04-003 plus the need for a cash view. It is noted in [D-P04-003](../DECISION-LOG.md#d-p04-003--q11-recognition-and-double-count-prevention).
- Payments must be fully allocated and cannot predate the obligation they settle (no advances in Phase 4).
- Currency validity follows the server runtime's ICU ISO 4217 list. Browser-side validation is advisory.
