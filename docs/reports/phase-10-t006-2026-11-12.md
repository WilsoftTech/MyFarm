**Who this is for:** the project owner and reviewers of Phase 10.

## What T006 adds

- **Batch page, "Feed for alerts" (D-P10-007).** The page shows which supply item the batch is fed from, or says none is chosen and that alerts cannot then say when feed is running low.
  - The farmer picks from the farm's supply items, each shown with its unit. An item not counted in kg is flagged on the spot, because it cannot be compared with feed given in kg.
  - The save reuses the existing form hook: the request ID survives a retry, the version is checked, and the button is disabled offline.
  - A closed batch cannot change its feed item.
  - With no supply items, the page links to adding one.
- **Alerts page.** Threshold alerts and their "not enough records" reasons arrived with T005; T006 verifies them in the browser.
- **Read of the feed item:** `FarmIntelligenceService.feedItem`, which refuses a batch of another farm.

## MYF-P10-AC002: "Thresholds and demand assumptions explicit; comparable units required"

| Spec check | Where it is verified |
|---|---|
| Threshold boundaries | Unit: exactly 1% deaths, 7 days of feed and cost equal to price are not raised, and the next step past each is raised (T004). Browser: 1.2% deaths and 6 days of feed are raised |
| Wrong units | Unit: a feed item counted in bags is not judged (T004). Browser: choosing "Maize bran (BAG)" shows the warning on the form, and the Alerts page says the item cannot be compared |
| Missing demand | Unit: no feed given, too few feed days and no feed item are not judged. Browser: before a feed item is chosen, the Alerts page says so |
| No-LLM execution | Unit: a scan of the farm-intelligence domain and contracts finds no model or network call (T004); the rules are plain code run on the server |
| Explicit thresholds and assumptions | Each alert prints its rule and version, the period, and its inputs: deaths of birds at risk; stock, daily use, days left and feed days per week |

The AC002 browser test lays out a batch fed 50 kg a day for 14 days, with 6 of its 500 birds dead in the last week. It checks, in order:
1. The Alerts page shows a Warning, "6 of 500 (1.2%)", with its rule, version and the vet or extension officer step.
2. The page says no feed item is chosen.
3. Choosing a bag-counted item on the batch page warns on the form. Once saved, the Alerts page says it cannot be compared.
4. Choosing "Layer mash (KG)", with 300 kg in stock, gives "Feed is running low": 300 kg, 50 kg a day, 6 days, feed given 7, 7, the 14-day period, and the rule with its version.
