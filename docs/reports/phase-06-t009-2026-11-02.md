Adds repeating activity list, start and detail screens with change-from-a-date and confirmed stop, plus a month calendar grouped by due date with previous and next month links. The activity lists, calendar and series pages run the idempotent expansion before reading, so due occurrences appear without a background job. The cancel form became a shared `ActivityConfirmForm` used for both cancellation and stopping a series; other screens reuse `RecordForm`, `ChoiceField`, `ProductionShell` and pagination.

MYF-P06-AC003 is verified locally. PASS: typecheck, lint, fixture production build, unit and component tests, and desktop/mobile browser tests: 30 upcoming activities with no repeats, unchanged after reopening every page and repeating expansion, a skipped date stays skipped, a change continues weekly from its date, a stop ends future entries, milking is shown as unavailable, weeding is not offered, input is retained offline, and foreign farms are denied.

Running expansion during a page load means a read page can write occurrences. It is idempotent, membership-checked and bounded, and there is no background job in this phase; the T010 audit records this.
