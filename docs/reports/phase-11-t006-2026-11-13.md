**Who this is for:** the project owner and reviewers of Phase 11.

## What T006 adds

- **Example questions.** Under the question box there's one example for each kind of question, each a button that fills the box, so a farmer need not type. The hint keeps the warning against names and phone numbers.
- **Where each answer came from, in the farmer's words:**

  | Question types | Source line |
  |---|---|
  | Money questions | "the money figures on the farm screen" |
  | Compare seasons | "…for this season and the one before" |
  | Ask records | "your money records" |
  | Unusual costs | "the farm's alerts, each over its own period" |
  | Upcoming tasks | "your planned farm work" |

  Each also shows the period, how money was counted and when it was calculated.
- **A link to the full page** under each answer: the farm screen, all money records, Alerts, Activities, or the money summary (where the report can be downloaded, under its own limit and audit). Links are part of the page and never part of an answer, because answers containing links are refused (T001).
- **Every figure kind displays plainly.** Counts are grouped (and say "or more" past a list's limit), percentages carry %, dates read as year-month-day, and amounts are grouped with their currency.
