## Changes

`PilotEvidenceService` (`src/modules/field-pilot/application/evidence.ts`) and its repository, for ADMIN staff of the running organization only:

- **Evidence view:** observations still usable as evidence (newest first, by code name), findings with the observations still behind them and the dates they span, past reviews, the decisions the evidence allows today, and the gate figures a review would record.
- **Observations** are recorded once per request; one about a farmer must name a farmer still in this cohort.
- **Findings** are checked against the observations that stand at that moment; closing is one-way with a version check.
- **Reviews:** the server works out the measures at the moment of review and refuses a decision the evidence does not allow (CONFLICT). The decision, reason, date and gate figures are stored together and never change.

Routes under `/api/v1/scopes/{scopeId}/pilot/cohorts/{cohortId}/`: `GET evidence`, `POST observations`, `POST findings`, `POST findings/{id}/close`, `POST reviews`.
