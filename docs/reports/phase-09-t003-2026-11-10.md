## Changes

**Farmers** (`/pilot`, linked as "Pilot" in the farmer menu). Before joining, the page shows every consent point in full, a code field and a box to tick; joining without ticking it is refused with a plain message. A wrong, closed or ended code gets one message, "That code was not accepted…", without saying which. Once in, the farmer sees the pilot name, their code name, the date they joined, what they agreed to, and a "Leave the pilot" form that says what leaving does and needs a clear yes.

**Staff** (`/admin/pilot?scope=…`, linked from the administrator workspace) list cohorts and open one. The cohort page shows the ten measures, the week-8 and end-of-pilot checks ("Not judged yet" until the week ends), a week-by-week table, and each farmer by code name with what happened each week ("Used", "Used after reminder", "Reminded", "No records"). Forms log reminders, incidents (with "do not write names or phone numbers") and resolutions, and record paper consent references. A share that cannot be worked out says why instead of showing 0. The screen calculates nothing.

**Found and fixed:** `/pilot` was not on the list of signed-in pages the request proxy protects, so an anonymous visitor reached the page and was only then sent to sign-in. The proxy now sends them straight to sign-in with a private redirect, like every other signed-in page.
