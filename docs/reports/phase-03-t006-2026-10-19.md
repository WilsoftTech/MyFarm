## AC002 oracle

| Oracle | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Date ordering | A harvest before planting is rejected in the form (component) and in the real app (browser); a corrected date saves |
| Missing optional variety | A cycle with no variety and no expected harvest saves; detail shows "Not recorded" and "Not recorded — open-ended cycle" |
| Area boundaries | Zero area is rejected before submit. Overprecise input normalizes (`00.100000` → `0.1`). A second cycle exceeding the 1-acre plot fails with no row written and the draft kept. With a reason it saves, and the reason is displayed |
| Units explicit | The unit follows the chosen plot ("Acres"/"Hectares"), is read-only, and is shown with every area on list and detail. Plots with unknown area cannot be chosen |

Additional browser coverage includes separate histories on two farms, offline-disabled saves that keep the draft, a lost-response retry that creates exactly one cycle and audit event, and IDOR checks (foreign farm detail and foreign enterprise preset, plus another user and ADMIN on all three routes). It also covers revocation after opening the form, which keeps the draft, catalog and insert permission outages that stay errors and recover with retry, pagination of choices and lists, and expired sessions. Desktop and Pixel 7 screenshots confirmed no horizontal scroll.
