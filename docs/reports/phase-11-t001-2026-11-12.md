## What T001 adds

`src/modules/ai-assistant/`, domain and contracts only:

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `domain/answer.ts` | Statements labeled FACT, INFERENCE, RECOMMENDATION or UNCERTAINTY (R003), and `answerProblems`, which checks every answer before it is shown |
| `domain/highest-profit.ts` | The R001 tool: Phase 8 enterprise gross margin arranged as evidence (scope, period, basis, time of calculation, currency, figures) and the plain answer built from it |
| `domain/explain-request.ts` | The only object an explainer receives, with names and IDs removed (D-P11-003) |
| `domain/policy.ts` | Q20 as decided, in one place |
| `contracts/assistant.ts` | Strict schemas for a question, a detected intent and an explained answer; the explainer and intent-detector ports |

**Main design decision: an explanation can't contain its own figures.** The explainer (a fake now, an AI later) writes words with placeholders: `{F1}` for a figure, `{E1}` for an enterprise, `{P}` for the period. MyFarm fills each one from its own calculation when the answer is shown. The check refuses an answer that:

- has a digit (any script), a percent sign or a spelled amount ("million", "percent");
- cites a ref the evidence lacks, or uses a placeholder it does not cite;
- contains a link or stray braces;
- makes a FACT or INFERENCE without a figure;
- makes any claim except UNCERTAINTY when the evidence allows no conclusion;
- concludes without citing the leading figure and enterprise (so it can't name the wrong winner).

A wrong number therefore cannot reach the farmer, which is the exit gate (P0645). When an explanation fails, the plain answer is shown instead (T002).

**The source example (P0624).** For a season where poultry's gross margin is UGX 1,420,000 and maize's is UGX 300,000, the answer is:

- FACT: Poultry made the most money for {season}: a gross margin of UGX 1,420,000.
- FACT: That is UGX 2,100,000 of sales less UGX 680,000 of costs linked to Poultry.
- FACT: Next was Maize, at UGX 300,000.
- UNCERTAINTY: records not linked to any enterprise are left out, with their amounts.
- UNCERTAINTY: gross margin is not net profit (shared costs, depreciation and loans are not taken off; D-P08-002).

Ties name every leader. When the highest gross margin is zero or less, the answer says no enterprise made money. Each currency is its own answer and is never combined (D-P04-002). With no records, or no money linked to an enterprise, the answer only says what is missing (AC001: no conclusion).

**Engineering readings for owner confirmation:**

1. "Most money" means the Phase 8 enterprise gross margin, the same figure as the farm screen, not net profit.
2. Season and enterprise names are farmers' own words, so they are not sent to a provider. The question itself is sent as typed, so the question box should tell farmers not to type names or phone numbers (D-P11-003).
3. Intent detection may return only an intent (`HIGHEST_PROFIT`, `AGRONOMY`, `UNSUPPORTED`). The period always comes from the farm screen's own filters, never from the question.

## Tests

`tests/unit/ai-assistant-domain.test.ts`, 16 tests:

- Golden: the source example, ties, no profit, two currencies, the enterprise limit.
- No conclusion: missing evidence.
- Numeric mismatch: digits in Latin, Arabic-Indic and superscript, "%", spelled amounts, naming the wrong winner.
- Prompt injection: invented refs, links, braces, extra `sql`/`write` fields, unknown statement kinds.
- Fake scope: tenant, farm, actor or role in a question, or scope in a detected intent.
- Two tenants: evidence is shown only for the farm it was calculated for.
- Privacy: no names, season labels or UUIDs in what is sent.
- Q20: the policy numbers, and a scan showing the module calls no network, provider SDK, database or environment secret.
