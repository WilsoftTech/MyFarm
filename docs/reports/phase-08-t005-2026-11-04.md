Adds `GET /api/v1/farms/{farmId}/profitability/metrics`, `FarmProfitabilityService.metrics` and `infrastructure/metric-facts-repository.ts`. Metrics share the dashboard's authorization and view rules. Viewing writes nothing, so no receipt or audit event applies.

**Batch link.** Expenses and purchases accept an optional `batchId`; income and sales refuse it. A cost on a batch takes that batch's enterprise, and naming a different enterprise is refused, so an enterprise's gross margin stays whole. This is normalized before the receipt check, otherwise a retry would look changed and be refused. Another farm's batch, another tenant's batch and a guessed ID all give `FORBIDDEN`. The record view gains `batchId` and `batchLabel`. Offline entries saved before this field simply lack it and still send.

**Reads.** A sale reaches its batch through its bird-sale event or egg stock operation. Reversed harvests, losses, deaths, purchases and sales are excluded by looking up their appended reversal. "Kg after recorded loss" subtracts both the loss recorded at harvest and later stock losses of that crop. Batch figures read all of a batch's records whichever view is chosen.

**Limits.** One screen reads at most 100 batches and 200 stock items, an engineering limit. Calculation is live and reads every money record on the farm, which has not been measured on long histories (P4-C3); T013 must address it.
