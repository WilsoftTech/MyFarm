## What T005 adds

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `prisma/migrations/202611120002_threshold_insights` | One additive migration in one transaction: the `BatchFeedItem` table, an optional crop-cycle subject on `FarmInsight` (exactly one subject), the new rule keys in the database check (crop loss only on crop cycles), and the live-key check covering both subjects |
| `supabase/policies/farm-intelligence-runtime.sql` | Insights may be added on an active batch or an active crop cycle. The feed link may be added or changed only by an active farmer member, for an active batch, to a supply item of the same farm |

| `src/components/intelligence/` | Threshold alerts and their "not enough records" reasons on the Alerts page |

**Records used.** Reversed entries, operations, harvests and records are left out, as in Phase 8.
- Each active batch's entries.
- All costs linked to each batch.
- Egg sales from the batch's egg stock in the last 28 days, and bird sales.
- The chosen feed item with its stock balance over every store.
- Feed given in the last 14 days, added up for every batch fed from the same item.
- Each active crop cycle's season costs (enterprise and season), its harvests less recorded loss, and the sales of its crop stock.

**Why the feed link is its own table.** Putting the link on `PoultryBatch` would need a new update policy on that table. Postgres combines permissive policies with OR, so the new policy could let other column updates bypass the batch-closure rules. A one-row-per-batch table keeps the link separate (D-P10-007: "one optional link on the batch"), and the batch's existing rights stay as they were.

**Crop loss and more than one cycle.** Season costs are linked to an enterprise and a season, not to a cycle. When that enterprise has more than one cycle that season, the costs cannot be split, so the rule is not judged and says why. This is the same caution as D-P08-010, applied to cycles.

**Screen.** Each threshold alert shows its inputs: deaths of birds at risk; stock, daily use and days left; or cost and price per unit with the totals behind them. It also shows the period ("the whole batch so far" or "this season so far" when there is no window), the rule with its version, and one next step. For high mortality the next step is D-P10-003's wording: have a vet or extension officer look at the flock. Figures that could not be compared are listed with their reasons, including "No feed item is chosen for this batch".

New integration tests, all on real records under the app's restricted role:
- **High mortality:** the golden batch's 12 of 495 birds in the last 7 days is a Warning with its inputs.
- **Feed running low:**
  - Before a feed item is chosen, the batch is listed as "no feed item".
  - Choosing it twice at once with one request gives one link; a second first choice is a conflict.
  - 300 kg at 50 kg a day gives a 6-day Warning. Restocking to 8 days clears it.
- **Feed item choice:** a bird stock item, an unknown item, an unknown batch, the farmer's other farm and an extra body field are all refused.
- **Loss per egg:** 100 UGX to produce against 50 UGX sold.
- **Loss per bird:** a meat batch at 5,000 UGX against 4,000 UGX; a laying batch is not judged per bird.
- **Loss per kg:** 560 UGX per kg kept against 550 UGX sold, stored as an insight about the crop cycle.
- **Database rights:** the app's role cannot link a non-supply item or delete a link, and the table refuses an insight about two subjects.

## Migration and deployment

Additive: one table and one nullable column. The `batchId` column of `FarmInsight` becomes nullable; every existing row keeps its value. Three checks are replaced with wider ones. Rollback: drop the table and column, and put back the T002 checks once no crop-cycle or new-rule insight exists. **Not applied to hosted development, not deployed.**
