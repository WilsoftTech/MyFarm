## Rules and limits

- Planted area is positive and fits the existing plot precision: up to 12 integer and 6 fractional digits. Commands remain decimal strings and normalize equivalent spellings (for example `000.500000` becomes `0.5`). Arithmetic uses native BigInt millionths without binary floating point. Zero-area plots may exist in the registry, but planting on one exceeds capacity and requires a reason.
- Expected harvest is optional, supporting perennial/open cycles. Dates must be real `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar dates; a specified harvest cannot precede planting. A season is a grouping label: no unapproved rule forces planting or harvest inside its date range.
- Enterprise/plot/season must match the authenticated farm/tenant and requested IDs; enterprise must be CROP, and enterprise/season must be ACTIVE. A mismatched or inactive catalog crop fails. These are deterministic parent checks, not authentication.
- Total planted area includes every ACTIVE cycle on the same plot, across enterprises and seasons. Closed cycles do not reserve area. Expected harvest is a forecast and never implicitly closes a cycle or frees area. A duplicate or foreign allocation row is rejected rather than silently double counted or filtered away.
- Plot area and unit must be known, and active allocations must use the same unit. A reason cannot bypass unknown area, a unit mismatch or scope checks. No conversion is performed. An unknown-area registry plot needs measured area recorded through an authorized workflow before planting can be validated; this task adds no plot-edit endpoint.
- Exceeding total plot capacity requires an explicit, nonblank recorded reason on that command (maximum 500 characters). The current model gives the authorized farm owner this responsibility; no separate reviewer or approval role is invented. A previous exception does not authorize a subsequent excess command automatically.
