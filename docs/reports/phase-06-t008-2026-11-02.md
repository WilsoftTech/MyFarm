Adds additive migration `202611020003_activity_recurrence`, the `ActivityRecurrence` model, series create, change, stop and expand services and routes under `/api/v1/farms/{farmId}/activity-series`. Occurrences are ordinary `ProductionActivity` rows, so each has its own lifecycle and appears in the normal lists.

A unique key on series and due date means repeated or concurrent expansion cannot create a second occurrence, and an advisory lock per series keeps a concurrent stop and expansion from leaving work scheduled past the stop date. Stopping cancels open occurrences from the chosen date with a reason and keeps earlier and completed history. A change closes the series and continues in a linked one from the change date, so earlier occurrences are untouched and dates already skipped stay skipped. A change date on or before a completed occurrence is refused. A series on a closed enterprise pauses until it is active again.

Security: membership is rechecked inside each write and after lock waits, foreign series and parents are refused with no receipt, and retries replay one result. The runtime role can update only series progress columns and cannot restart a stopped series. Generated occurrences carry no individual audit receipt because the series command is audited; this is a limitation for the phase audit. The tests also found and fixed a defect where expansion changed `updatedAt`, which made a replayed create return a different record.

Applied only to a disposable PostgreSQL 17 database. Not applied to hosted. The screens follow in T009.
