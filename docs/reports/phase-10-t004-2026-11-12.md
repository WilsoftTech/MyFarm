## What T004 adds

`src/modules/farm-intelligence/domain/thresholds.ts`, the R002 rules in the catalog, a threshold evidence contract and 13 tests. All five rules are **Warnings**: a figure is past an explicit limit, not only moving.

| Rule | Figure | Raised when | Not judged when |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH_MORTALITY (D-P10-003) | Deaths in the last 7 complete farm days ÷ (birds alive at their start + birds bought in them) | above 1% (exactly 1% is not raised) | under 20 birds; batch younger than the week; records on fewer than 4 of the 7 days |
| FEED_RUNNING_LOW (D-P10-005, D-P10-007) | Stock of the batch's chosen feed item ÷ average daily feed given over the last 14 complete days | under 7 days left | no feed item chosen; item not in kg; feed given on fewer than 4 days in either week; no feed given |
| LOSS_PER_EGG (D-P10-005) | Batch costs ÷ eggs laid, against egg sales ÷ eggs sold, last 28 complete days | cost per egg above price per egg | nothing laid; nothing sold in that currency |
| LOSS_PER_BIRD (D-P10-005, D-P08-006) | Batch costs so far ÷ birds started (Phase 8 cost per bird), against bird sales ÷ birds sold | cost per bird above price per bird | no birds; none sold in that currency |
| LOSS_PER_KG (D-P10-005, D-P08-005) | Season costs ÷ kg kept (Phase 8 cost per kg, the break-even price), against crop sales ÷ kg sold | cost per kg above price per kg | nothing kept; none sold in that currency; the enterprise grew more than one crop that season (D-P08-010) |

Every comparison is exact (cross-multiplied before any rounding), within one currency and one unit. Evidence stores:
- the window, or none when the rule covers a whole batch life or season;
- the named inputs, such as deaths and birds at risk, stock, daily use and days left, or costs, units, cost per unit, sales, units sold and price per unit;
- the exact figure compared, so a later evaluation can tell whether it got worse.

The repeat rule from T001 now covers thresholds: fewer days of feed left is worse, and more deaths or cost further above price is worse.

## Engineering readings for owner confirmation

These fill gaps the decisions left open. Each is easy to change:

1. **High mortality counts birds bought during the week** as at risk. Without them, a purchase in the week would make the rate look higher than it is.
2. **Shared feed.** Batches fed from the same item count their feed use together. Judging each batch alone would miss a store that two flocks are emptying.
3. **Bird loss is for meat batches** (purpose MEAT). A laying batch's costs go mostly into eggs, so its cost per bird would always look like a loss against the price of a spent hen. Egg loss covers layers.
4. **Egg loss uses all costs linked to the batch** in the 28 days, as D-P10-005 says. A bird purchase inside those days is included, and the evidence shows the costs so the farmer can see why.
5. **Loss rules need no daily-record coverage.** They need production and sales in the same currency; the 4-of-7-days coverage in D-P10-004 was written for the trends.

The new tests cover:
- **Mortality:** exactly 1% not raised and 1.2% raised; birds bought in the week; thin, small or young batches.
- **Feed:** exactly 7 days not raised; a fraction under 7 days and empty stock raised; shared use. Wrong units (a feed item counted in bags), missing demand (no feed given), no item and too few feed days are not guessed.
- **Eggs:** cost equal to price not raised, a shilling below price raised; window and currency separation.
- **Crops:** the Phase 8 example, 560 UGX per kg against sales at 550, is a loss; more than one crop is blocked.
- **Missing inputs:** nothing produced, nothing sold, a negative amount refused.
- **Evidence and repeats:** the evidence round trip, a refused unknown rule, and the repeat directions.
- **No-LLM check:** a scan finds no model or network call in the farm-intelligence domain and contracts.
