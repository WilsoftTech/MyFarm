## Changes

`farmScreen(farmId, filters)` runs the dashboard, metrics and trends for the same view and adds `reconcile` from T010. Each part authorizes and validates before it reads, so a denied or invalid request reads nothing; the unit test proves no source is read for a denied farm. Figures are calculated on each request (D-P08-008). No endpoint is added: the screen is the only caller, and each part already has its own endpoint.

`tests/integration/mvp-journey.test.ts` is the AC004 regression on real records under the restricted role. It uses the real `PushService` and existing services, as a phone would:

1. An offline batch records the fertilizer cost (2,100,000 UGX), the harvest (4,000 kg, 250 kg lost) and other income (300,000 UGX). All three commit.
2. The reply is lost, so the same batch is sent again, and again twice at once. Each reply equals the first, and the farm gains exactly 2 money records, 1 harvest, 2 stock movements and 3 receipts.
3. A batch is interrupted: a labour cost commits and the next entry is refused. The retried cost does not repeat.
4. 1,000 kg is sold online for 1,200,000 UGX.

The farm screen then shows exactly: earned 1,500,000, spent 2,150,000, recorded surplus −650,000 UGX; 3 acres, 3,750 kg kept, 1,333.33 kg per acre and 573.33 UGX per kg; labour 50,000 and inputs 2,100,000; 2,750 kg left in stock, estimated at 3,300,000 UGX. Reconciliation passes. After the labour cost is reversed it still passes in the season, cash, all-records and date views. Another farm is refused on screen and offline, and nothing changes.
