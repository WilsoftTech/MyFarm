Adds `domain/sync-state.ts`: the six source states with only the designed moves allowed, retry backoff from 5 seconds to 15 minutes with jitter, leases that recover a send abandoned by a crash, oldest-first ordering, dependency holds, pruning and discard chains. CONFLICT and FAILED leave only through a new command with a new ID, so a key is never reused for a changed intent.

A refused entry keeps its details for repair, a stale version keeps the current record for review, and a blocked child stays unsent rather than failing. The 15-minute cap, 2-minute lease and 7-day retention of sent entries are engineering choices open to owner review.
