## Changes

From a cohort page, "Observations and decision" (`/admin/pilot/{cohortId}/evidence?scope=…`) opens a page that states downloads do not count as evidence, and has three parts:

- **Observations:** a form for date, farmer (code name, or none), where (expense form, offline work…), what happened (hesitated, words not understood…) and what was seen, with no names; then the list, newest first.
- **Findings:** a one-sentence finding plus ticks on the observations behind it; saving without a tick is refused. Each finding shows how many observations still support it and the dates they span, or "No evidence left" once the farmers it was about have left. Open findings can be closed as acted on or dismissed.
- **Decision:** the current check in words ("Week 8 check: 0 of 1 farmers kept using MyFarm without a reminder. That is less than half."). Only the decisions the evidence allows are offered, plus a reason; past decisions list the figures they were recorded with.

**Found and fixed:** after a save, staff forms redirected to the same page and kept what had just been saved. A second observation was silently recorded about the same farmer. Each save now adds a fresh marker to the address, and the forms start empty; this applies to the reminder and incident forms on the cohort page too.
