## Changes

`src/modules/field-pilot/domain/measures.ts` works out the ten measures from plain facts: receipts, usage log rows, prompts and incidents. Every row is unique per request and deduplicated again, so a retried entry counts once. Only joined farmers count, only from the day they joined, only inside the window and up to today. A withdrawn farmer counts nowhere (D-P09-009). Shares that cannot be worked out say why ("no farmers yet", "no entries from phones") instead of 0. The gate is checked at week 8 and at the last week, each only once that week has ended (D-P09-004).

`domain/consent.ts` holds the consent words, with a version recorded on each participation. **They are a draft:** the owner must approve them, in every language farmers will read, before a real farmer joins. That is now on the pilot-start checklist.

`contracts/pilot.ts` validates joining, withdrawal, cohorts, paper consent, prompts and incidents. Actor, tenant, cohort ownership and pseudonym are never accepted from a client. A cohort runs for one season, at most 200 days. Codes forgive spaces, dashes and case.

Five tables (`PilotCohort`, `PilotParticipation`, `PilotUsageEvent`, `PilotPrompt`, `SupportIncident`), six enums, checks on code and pseudonym format, status consistency and dates, and RLS enabled. The migration is one transaction; a test proves a failure leaves nothing behind. `supabase/policies/field-pilot-runtime.sql` joins the manifest before browser lockdown:

- Only an active ADMIN of an ORGANIZATION tenant opens a cohort, logs prompts and incidents, or records paper consent.
- Only an active farmer joins, only an active cohort, and only under its current consent version.
- Withdrawal and resolving an incident are one-way. A withdrawn participation leaves every update policy, so it cannot be re-enrolled, prompted or given paper consent.
- The usage log accepts only a member acting in their own tenant, once per entry. Nothing in these tables can be deleted by the application.

Records are counted from the existing receipts, so no farm table changes. Staff tables live in the staff organization's tenant; a participation points at the farmer's user, not at any farm record.

## Choices to review

- **What counts as a record:** money records and payments, harvests, stock operations, poultry production, and activities scheduled, done or completed. Setup (farm, plot, enterprise, season), reversals and photos do not. A record reversed later still counts as usage.
- **Weeks** start on the cohort's start date in farm days (Africa/Kampala), not on a calendar weekday.
- **Late joiners** count in the week-8 denominator, so the gate is strict: a farmer who joined in week 6 cannot be retained by week 8.
- **Completed cycle:** closing a crop cycle or poultry batch in the window. Whether it had a harvest is not checked.
- **No re-joining** after withdrawal.
