## What T007 adds

The source asks the assistant to keep FACT, INFERENCE, RECOMMENDATION and UNCERTAINTY apart, "particularly for agronomic advice" (P0634-P0643). T007 makes each label checkable. Every answer, the explainer's or MyFarm's own, now also fails on any of these:

| Problem | When |
|---|---|
| `UNSAFE_CERTAINTY` | An inference, suggestion or uncertainty uses certainty words: definitely, certainly, guaranteed, always, for sure, no doubt, proven… |
| `FUTURE_AS_FACT` | A fact predicts or claims certainty: will, going to, next season, expected, forecast… A fact is only what the records say |
| `AGRONOMY_NOT_UNCERTAIN` | Anything but an uncertainty mentions spraying, fertilizer, pesticides, vaccines, medicine, doses, treatment and the like. Advice is only ever "Not certain", with the extension-officer referral (D-P11-005) |
| `CONFLICT_STATED_AS_FACT` | A fact, inference or suggestion cites a figure MyFarm's own checks disagree on |
| `CONFLICT_NOT_DISCLOSED` | Some figures disagree and no uncertainty says so, citing them |

The word lists are deliberately broad. A refused explanation costs the farmer nothing, because MyFarm's own plain answer is shown instead.

**Contradictory sources** (`withDisagreements`). The farm screen already checks that its totals add up by two different paths (Phase 8 reconciliation). When a check fails in a currency:
- every money figure in that currency becomes a conflict;
- the two disagreeing totals are added to the evidence;
- the answer has no conclusion, and its plain answer says "MyFarm's own checks do not add up for 2026A in this currency: one way of adding the records gives …, another gives …. Until this is put right, MyFarm gives none of these figures as a fact."

Other currencies are unaffected.

**Groundedness evaluation** (`domain/groundedness.ts`). Given what the farmer was shown and MyFarm's own evidence worked out again independently, it counts:
- **Figures matching:** each shown figure checked against that independent calculation (measure and exact value); a digit in the words counts as a wrong figure.
- **Cross-farm leaks:** any other farm's name or amount in the answer.
- **Injections refused:** whether any injected words got through.

`meetsGate` applies the owner's gate: 100% of figures match, 0 cross-farm leaks, and every injection refused.

## Tests

`tests/unit/ai-assistant-labels.test.ts`, 6 tests:

- **Certainty:** "Poultry will definitely keep paying off" as an inference, "Always keep…" as a suggestion and "guaranteed to hold next season" as an uncertainty are refused. "You will earn more next season" and "expected to be" as facts are refused. The plain inference "Going by these records, more money went out than came in" passes.
- **Advice:** spraying, pesticide and vaccination advice is refused as a fact, inference or suggestion, and allowed only as an uncertainty pointing to an extension officer.
- **Disagreement:** a failed check in UGX gives no conclusion, marks every UGX money figure, and shows both totals; USD stays answered. An explanation stating the surplus as fact, or hiding the disagreement, is refused. Every money tool's plain answer under disagreement passes.
- **Groundedness:** MyFarm's own answers for every question type (over 40 figures) meet the gate. One changed figure (1,420,001 against 1,420,000), another farm's name, an injected phrase that got through, or a number typed into the words each fails it.

Every earlier test of the plain answers now also passes these rules (they run through the same checks).
