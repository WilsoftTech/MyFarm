## What T002 adds

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `prisma/migrations/202611120001_farm_insights` | One additive migration in one transaction: the `FarmInsight` table, its enums and checks, row security, and a `metric` marker on `TransactionCategory` |
| `supabase/policies/farm-intelligence-runtime.sql` | What the app's role may do with insights; in the provisioning list before browser lockdown |
| `src/modules/farm-intelligence/infrastructure/insight-repository.ts` | Gathers each active batch's records, applies the T001 rules, and stores or clears insights in one transaction |
| `src/modules/farm-intelligence/application/insights.ts` | Authorizes the farm, then runs the rules on the farm's date (Africa/Kampala) |
| `GET /api/v1/farms/{farmId}/insights` | Live insights, Warnings first, plus every figure that could not be judged and why |
| `POST /api/v1/farms/{farmId}/insights/{insightId}/acknowledge` | `{ requestId, expectedVersion }`; retry-safe and version-checked |

**Records used.**
- **Entries:** each active batch's production entries up to the end of the current window, leaving out entries reversed through stock reversal.
- **Costs:** posted expense and purchase records linked to the batch whose category is marked as feed.
- **Limit:** at most 50 active batches per farm, newest first; the feed reports when more were left out.

**Feed category as data.** An existing guard (P04-T004) keeps code from naming a seeded category or using its ID. Following the Phase 8 cost groups (D-P08-009), the migration adds a nullable `metric` marker to categories and sets it to `FEED` on the approved Feed category; the code reads the marker. A check keeps the marker off revenue categories.

**Storage and repeats.**
- One live insight per batch, rule and currency. While an insight is open or acknowledged, it holds a `liveKey`, and a unique index on that key stops two requests at the same moment from raising it twice. A check ties the key to the batch, rule and currency.
- Clearing removes the key, so a worse figure after acknowledgement can be raised in its place.
- An insight on a batch that has been closed is cleared, since a closed batch has no current trend.

**Opening the feed writes.** `GET` evaluates and stores. The result depends only on the farm's records and date, so repeating it changes nothing. The feed has no separate "run" step to forget.

**Who may do what.**
- The feed and acknowledgement need an active farmer member of the farm, checked again inside the transaction.
- An insight id from another farm, or one reached through another farm's path, is refused as forbidden.
- Acknowledgement claims a command receipt, which is also its audit event (`INSIGHT_ACKNOWLEDGED`).

**Database rights.** The app's role may:
- read insights;
- add only open, unacknowledged insights on active batches of the same farm;
- change only status, live key, acknowledgement, clearing and version. Acknowledging also requires an active farmer member of that farm.

It cannot change the rule, its version or the evidence, cannot delete an insight, and cannot reopen a cleared one.

The 11 integration tests run under the app's restricted role and check:
- The source's example end to end: one Warning for feed +24% and eggs −12%, a Watch for deaths +1.4 points, with the windows, coverage and values.
- Opening the feed again, three times at once, and for a new batch twice at once: one live insight per rule.
- Acknowledging twice at once with one request ID gives one result and one audit event. A stale version or a reused ID with a different version is a conflict, and an acknowledged insight is not raised again.
- A reversed death entry is left out. Real extra deaths after acknowledgement raise a new insight (3.4%, +2.4 points) and clear the old one.
- Thin records list what is missing.
- Closing a batch clears its insights.
- A guessed farm, a guessed insight, an insight reached through the farmer's other farm, an extra body field, and a member who left the farm are all refused.
- The migration marks only Feed, and a revenue category cannot take the marker.
- The app's role cannot change the evidence or rule, delete, reopen a cleared insight, or add an acknowledged one. The table refuses a live key that does not match its insight.

Browser suites were not run: no page changed. The screens and their browser tests come in T003.
