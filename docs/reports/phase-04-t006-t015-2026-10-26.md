## Instruction and decisions

The owner instructed "implement T006 -T016. COMMIT AFTER PHASE 04 COMPLETE" and answered four open rules before any code was written:

| Decision | Rule |
|---|---|
| [D-P04-007](../DECISION-LOG.md#d-p04-007--r003-crop-cycle-matched-links) | All links stay on the same farm. A CROP enterprise linked together with a plot and/or season must match one of its crop cycles (any status). Other enterprise types only need same-farm links. Activity links remain Phase 6. |
| [D-P04-008](../DECISION-LOG.md#d-p04-008--r004-enterprise-gross-margin) | Enterprise gross margin is linked revenue minus linked costs. The farm figure is the sum over enterprises. Unlinked revenue and costs are shown separately and never deducted. |
| [D-P04-009](../DECISION-LOG.md#d-p04-009--r005-csv-export) | CSV export of the money summary for active farm members only; others are denied. |
| [D-P04-010](../DECISION-LOG.md#d-p04-010--report-filters) | Reports filter by an inclusive date range and by basis (accrual or cash), and state basis, range and as-of time. |

Unlike T001–T005, these tasks were not committed one by one: one commit follows phase completion (D-P04-011).
