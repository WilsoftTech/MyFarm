## Changes

`src/modules/field-pilot/domain/evidence.ts`:

- **Observations** are dated notes by staff: which screen (expense form, harvest form, offline work…), what kind (hesitation, confusion, error, workaround, wording, positive), and a short note with no names. They may be about one farmer, by code name, or general.
- **Opt-out removal:** an observation about a farmer who withdrew stops being evidence and disappears from staff views, as their measures do (D-P09-009). General observations stay.
- **Findings** must cite at least one usable observation, each once. A finding whose only observations were about farmers who left shows that it has no evidence left, with no dates.
- **Decisions (D-P09-004):** "continue" is always allowed. A decision either way waits until the week-8 check has been judged. **GO** needs that check met, and the end check met too once it has been judged. A review records the latest judged gate figures as the server worked them out.

Contracts take a decision and a reason only. They reject downloads, installs, a client's own gate figures and a review date, so evidence can only come from observation and the server's measures.

A database check refuses a GO without a met gate, a decision either way without a judged gate, and retained farmers above those onboarded. `field-pilot-runtime.sql` adds the policies: staff only; evidence links only within one cohort; closing a finding is one-way; reviews are append-only and never change. Nothing can be deleted by the application.

## Choices to review

- **Who decides:** a review is recorded by pilot staff (ADMIN of the running organization). The owner's acceptance of the phase stays separate.
- **Withdrawn farmers' observations** are kept in the database but leave every view and stop counting as evidence, matching D-P09-009 rather than deleting history.
- **Screens and kinds** are fixed lists, so findings can be grouped; "Other" exists for anything else.
