## Delivered scope

MYF-P12-T001 is verified. Phase 12 is 1/10 (10%). R001 is partially implemented; full AC001 remains pending the authorized application service and UI. T002-T010 and AC002/AC003 are not complete.

Files: src/modules/voice-experience/contracts/voice.ts, domain/expense-review.ts, infrastructure/fake-intent-adapter.ts; tests/unit/voice-expense-review.test.ts; existing tests/unit/mvp-scope.test.ts adjusted for the authorized voice phase. No new UI, endpoint, persistent model or financial write.

The fake recognizes exactly the source fixture, two bags of chicken feed for 180,000, producing an untrusted proposal. It is not a speech recognizer or general language parser. All other inputs return null. Review uses a total of UGX 180000 and retains two bags only as descriptive context. It never converts bags to kg, multiplies the total by bags, or changes inventory.

Reused the strict Phase 4 expense schema, currency normalization, exact decimal money validation, UUID/date validation and FoundationError. The provider has no category/date/farm/actor/tenant fields. Category and date require selection and must be resolved under current farm authorization by T002. Confirmation accepts only the boolean true in its separate input shape; that shape is not an executable authorization grant. Binding actor/scope/payload/expiry and idempotent execution are future required service work, not claimed by this domain test.
