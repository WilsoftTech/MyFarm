## What T009 adds

- **"What the labels mean"**, an expandable guide under the question box. It says what each label promises:

  | Label | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Fact from your records | Exactly what your records say: a figure, a date or an entry. Nothing is guessed. |
  | What this suggests | A reading of the facts. It is not certain; check it against your own knowledge of the farm. |
  | Suggestion | Something you might do. It is never certain. |
  | Not certain | Something MyFarm cannot know from your records, or advice only an extension officer should give. |

  It adds that MyFarm works out every figure and that the wording is checked before the farmer sees it.
- **Figures MyFarm's own checks disagree on** carry "(does not add up)" wherever they appear. They only ever appear in "Not certain" statements (T007).
- **Labels stay readable text**, not colors alone: a filled badge for facts and an outlined badge for "Not certain". They are read out by screen readers as part of each statement.
