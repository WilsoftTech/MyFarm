## Changes

The page now makes one `farmScreen` call instead of three separate ones. `src/components/analytics/reconcile.tsx` shows nothing while the figures add up. If a check ever fails, it says "These figures do not add up", tells the farmer not to rely on the page for now and that their records are not changed, and lists each failed check in plain words with the expected and found amounts. It recalculates nothing. Every check has plain wording, which a test enforces.

## Source farmer journey (AC004), in the browser

On a signed-in phone and desktop: a season, plot, maize enterprise, store and a 3-acre crop cycle are set up online. With the app server stopped, the farmer records the 2,100,000 UGX fertilizer expense for maize and the 4,000 kg harvest with 250 kg lost on `/offline`. After reconnecting, the first send is cut off after the server has the batch, and the farmer presses "Send now" again. Everything sends, and exactly one 2,100,000 UGX expense exists. The farm screen then shows recorded surplus UGX −2,100,000, 3,750 kg kept, 1,333.33 kg per acre, UGX 700,000 per acre and UGX 560 per kg, with no reconciliation warning and no sideways scrolling. `/assistant`, `/marketplace`, `/wallet` and `/loans` return 404.
