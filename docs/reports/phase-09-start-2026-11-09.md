Phase 9 is a field pilot: about 25 consenting farmers using MyFarm for one season while the team watches whether they keep entering records without being pushed. The software this phase builds lets the team run that pilot honestly. **The exit gate needs real farmers' behaviour over weeks, which no code or test can produce.** The tasks can be completed here; the gate cannot until the real pilot runs.

## What gets built

- **Joining (D-P09-006):** a signed-in farmer enters a cohort code, reads plain consent text and joins under a pseudonym (P-01, P-02…). They can withdraw in the app at any time; from then on none of their activity counts anywhere (D-P09-009).
- **Pilot staff view (D-P09-007):** ADMIN members of the organization running the cohort see the ten measures and, per pseudonym, weekly activity counts. Never names, phones, money amounts or records.
- **Logs staff keep:** support incidents, prompts given to a farmer (a prompted week does not count as voluntary, D-P09-004), and, in R002, dated observations of where farmers hesitate, and the dated go/no-go reviews.
- **Feature freeze (P0541):** no new farmer-facing capability; only what the pilot needs, plus fixes.

## The ten measures (R001)

Weeks run from the cohort's start date in farm days (Africa/Kampala). Every count comes from a server receipt or a log row that is unique per request, so a retried entry is counted once. A withdrawn farmer is left out of all of them.

| # | Measure | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farmers onboarded | Participants currently joined |
| 2 | Farmers active after onboarding | Retained voluntarily in the week: active, unprompted, in at least 3 of the last 4 weeks (D-P09-003, D-P09-004), ÷ farmers onboarded |
| 3 | Records per farmer per week | Records saved in the week ÷ farmers joined by then |
| 4 | Expenses recorded | Expense and purchase records |
| 5 | Harvests recorded | Harvest records |
| 6 | Tasks completed | Activities completed or recorded as done |
| 7 | Offline transactions | Entries committed from a phone's offline queue (D-P09-008) |
| 8 | Sync failures | Entries the server refused, and their share of offline entries; attempts that never reached the server are not seen |
| 9 | Support incidents | Incidents logged by staff, by severity, with how many are open |
| 10 | Farmers completing a production cycle | Farmers who closed at least one crop cycle or poultry batch ÷ farmers onboarded |

A "record" is a saved money record or payment, harvest, stock operation, poultry production entry, or activity scheduled, done or completed. Setting up a farm, plot, enterprise or season, reversals and photos are not counted as records. Records reversed later still count, because the farmer did enter them.
