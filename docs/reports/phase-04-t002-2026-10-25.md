## Schema

- **Catalogs:**
  - `TransactionCategory` has a kind (COST/REVENUE), a name unique per kind, and an `active` flag. It holds the 13 source cost categories and the 4 approved revenue categories.
  - `PaymentMethod` holds Cash, Mobile Money and Bank transfer.
  - Both are global and read-only to the runtime role. Changes happen only by reviewed data-release migration.
- **`AccountingRecord`:** one table for EXPENSE/INCOME/PURCHASE/SALE.
  - Each record is owned by a farm through a composite `(farmId, tenantId)` FK.
  - It stores the amount as numeric(20,4), a three-letter uppercase currency, a date, an optional counterparty and a snapshot of the category name.
  - The payment method is optional and allowed only on EXPENSE/INCOME (CHECK). Plot, enterprise and season links are optional.
  - Prisma cannot express an optional relation over the required `farmId`/`tenantId`, so these links are single-column FKs. Same-farm consistency is enforced by the service and by the runtime INSERT policy.
- **`PaymentRecord` and `PaymentAllocation`:** a payment stores a direction, amount, currency, date, method and optional reference. Each allocation references both its payment and its obligation through composite `(id, farmId, tenantId)` FKs, and is unique per pair. An allocation has no status of its own; it follows its payment's status, so reversing a payment reverses its allocations.
- **`AccountingReversal`:** append-only. Exactly one of `recordId` or `paymentId` is set (CHECK), each is unique (one reversal per target), and every reversal stores a reason and actor.
- **CHECKs and RLS:** amounts > 0, currency format, text lengths, version ≥ 1, date range. Deny-all RLS is on every new table before grants.

## Runtime policy

- **Reads:** `myfarm_runtime` gets SELECT on the catalogs and SELECT/INSERT on the four accounting tables.
- **Updates:** limited to the columns `status`, `version` and `updatedAt` on records and payments. No DELETE, no catalog writes and no browser grants.
- **INSERT policies** recheck that the creator is an active farmer member of the farm and that the record starts POSTED/version 1. They also check:
  - the category is active and of the right kind, and its name matches the snapshot
  - the payment method, if any, is active
  - every link is on the same farm
  - an allocation's payment and obligation are POSTED, on the same farm and in the same currency, with a matching direction/kind and the payment dated on or after the obligation
  - a reversal's target is POSTED on the same farm
- **UPDATE policies** allow only POSTED → REVERSED, and only when a reversal row exists. A record cannot be reversed while it has POSTED allocations.

## Service and API

`FarmAccountingService` authorizes the current farm membership before parsing any input. Tenant, farm and actor come only from that authorization.

Every write:
- runs in one transaction with an atomic audit receipt (`ACCOUNTING_<KIND>_RECORDED`, `ACCOUNTING_PAYMENT_RECORDED` or `ACCOUNTING_RECORD_REVERSED`)
- rechecks membership after each wait
- replays an identical normalized retry
- returns CONFLICT for a changed retry, or for the same request on another farm

Payments and reversals take per-obligation advisory locks in ascending ID order, so concurrent payments cannot exceed what is outstanding. A second concurrent reversal also conflicts, which the unique target index enforces as well. A link to another farm or to an unknown ID is FORBIDDEN. An unknown or wrong-kind category or payment method is INVALID_INPUT, and an archived one is CONFLICT.

Endpoints under `/api/v1/farms/{farmId}/accounting/`:

| Method and path | Result |
|---|---|
| `GET categories?kind=COST\|REVENUE`, `GET payment-methods` | Active catalogs, paginated |
| `GET records`, `GET records/{recordId}` | `{ record }` with the derived `balance` for a sale/purchase |
| `POST expenses`, `income`, `sales`, `purchases` | 201 `{ record }` |
| `POST payments`, `GET payments/{paymentId}` | 201/200 `{ payment }` |
| `POST reversals` | 201 `{ reversal }` |

Error codes:
- **400:** invalid money, currency or date, forged fields, an unbalanced payment
- **403:** a foreign farm or ID, cross-origin requests
- **409:** a changed replay, overpayment, a second reversal, reversing a sale that still has payments
- **401:** no session or a revoked session

Responses are private and no-store.

## Limits and next step

- **Hosted dev:** the migration and `farm-accounting-runtime.sql` are local only. Applying them to hosted dev needs separate authorization (as for D-P03-015).
- **Over-allocation:** prevented by locks in the application. The RLS backstop checks direction, currency, date and status, but not the running sum.
- **Attachments:** deferred under the inherited retention/export condition.
- **Not yet built:** no listing of payments by farm, no category administration, no reports or totals (R004/R005).
