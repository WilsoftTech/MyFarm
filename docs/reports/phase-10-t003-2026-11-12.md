## What T003 adds

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `src/app/farms/[farmId]/insights/` | The Alerts page, its loading state, and the mark-as-seen server action |
| `src/components/intelligence/` | The feed, each alert card, the mark-as-seen form and plain-word labels |
| `src/components/production/shell.tsx`, farm page | An "Alerts" link in the production navigation and on the farm page |

**Each alert shows (MYF-P10-AC001):**
- the level: Warning or Watch, as text and not by colour alone;
- a plain title, such as "Fewer eggs while feed costs more", and the batch;
- each figure before and now, with the change: a percentage for feed and eggs, percentage points for deaths;
- both 4-week periods with their dates;
- days with records in each week, and the birds at the start of each period;
- the rule in plain words with its version, and the farm date it was worked out on.

The only suggestion is "Check what changed on this batch in the last 4 weeks." The rules are not advice, and no other recommendation was approved.

**Marking as seen.** An open alert has an "I have seen this" button. It reuses the existing production form hook:
- The request ID survives a retry.
- The server checks the version the farmer saw.
- Offline, the button is disabled and the usual offline message shows.

Once the alert is marked, the page says when, and that the alert comes back only if the figure gets worse.

**Empty and thin states.** A farm with no active batch says so. A farm whose batches show no change past the rules says "No alerts." Every figure that could not be judged is listed with its reasons in plain words, for example "No eggs were recorded in the earlier 4 weeks."

**Offline.** The page needs a connection to load, as the money reports do. Alerts are worked out on the server from synced records, so an offline phone cannot work them out (offline-sync architecture: operator data shows as-of or needs the network). An open page stays readable offline, and marking an alert as seen waits for a connection.
