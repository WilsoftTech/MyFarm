Adds additive migration `202611020002_activity_completion`, completion and cancellation services with routes, and bucketed lists for today, upcoming, missed, completed and cancelled. The worker is optional free text, and the signed-in member and server time are recorded as who completed it and when. A cost link reads its amount from the accounting record; the activity stores no money, and a later reversal shows on the activity.

Security and integrity: retries replay one result, a stale or concurrent second finish conflicts, membership is rechecked inside the transaction, and foreign activities and cost records are refused with no receipt. The runtime role can update only lifecycle columns and only while an activity is scheduled, so finished records cannot be reopened or rewritten by the application role. A database check keeps status and its who/when columns consistent.

PASS: typecheck, lint, 30 activity PostgreSQL tests including Africa/Kampala boundaries with a controlled clock, schema drift. Applied only to a disposable database. Not applied to hosted.
