## Interview guide

1. Show how you record expenses and what gets missed.
2. How do you estimate actual production costs?
3. How do you determine profit and which costs count?
4. Which records do you keep and where?
5. What records are forgotten and why?
6. Who manages/records/approves farm transactions?
7. How do you plan and assign activities?
8. How do you sell, weigh, grade and collect payment?
9. What information does a SACCO ask for?
10. Which smartphone/browser is available; is it shared?
11. How reliable are connectivity, power and charging?
12. Which language do you prefer for entry/explanations?
13. How comfortable are typing and voice; which accessibility needs apply?
14. How often is mobile money used, and how are fees/partial payments recorded?

Observe one actual record-entry and one production-cycle reconciliation where consent allows. Request anonymized examples rather than copies of IDs/bank statements. Record date, pseudonym, enterprise/device, observation vs inference, consent reference and unresolved questions; restrict raw notes outside public product documents.
