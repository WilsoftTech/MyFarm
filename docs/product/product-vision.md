MyFarm is a digital agricultural operating system that helps farmers record, understand, manage and improve farming businesses. Uganda is the initial market; global expansion requires configurable currency, units, language, location and agricultural catalogs rather than Uganda-specific business code.

The first value proposition is trusted records plus production/cost visibility, basic management and offline use. Poultry provides frequent expense, mortality, feed, egg and sale records. Crop cycles exercise plots, inputs, labour, harvest and stock. These are source recommendations adopted as the requested initial focus, not validated market findings.

The source evolution is records → accounting → management → intelligence → AI → cooperative/agribusiness → markets → payments → credit/insurance → ecosystem. Farmer managers and officers are secondary initial personas; cooperatives, buyers, SACCOs, finance providers, NGOs, suppliers, processors and exporters come later.

Success means farmers return voluntarily to maintain records and make better informed decisions. No adoption/revenue claim exists yet. [Field research](field-research.md) and [Phase 9](../phases/phase-09-field-pilot.md) must provide evidence.
