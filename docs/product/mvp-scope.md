MVP ends at Phase 8. Phase 0 research validates scope; documentation does not close discovery.

| Phase | Included behavior | MVP boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Secure typed modular foundation, auth, CI, migrations/quality/logs | Shells and interfaces; no full officer/admin commercial platform |
| 2 | Farmer/profile/farm/plot/membership/contact/document registry | Purposeful minimal personal data, optional GPS |
| 3 | Crop cycles, poultry batches, multiple farms/enterprises/seasons | Other enterprise types extensible, dedicated workflows deferred |
| 4 | Expense/income/sale/purchase/obligations/payment records/categories/files | Deterministic gross margin; payment method is bookkeeping |
| 5 | Harvest, stock ledger/storage/adjustment and poultry events | Reconstructable quantities; valuation policy unresolved |
| 6 | Activities/tasks/calendar/recurrence | Activity costs linked to accounting |
| 7 | Offline entries/photos/cache and idempotent sync | Supported device/duration policy must be approved |
| 8 | Enterprise profitability/yield/unit/bird metrics and trends | Authoritative numbers from deterministic services |

Usable MVP journey: farmer registers → creates farm/plots → starts crop season or poultry batch → records money/production/tasks → works offline → reconnects once → reviews reconciled scope totals. Phase 9 tests real usage.

AI/voice/weather, institutional operations, marketplace, provider payments/wallet, economic-profile sharing, partner lending/insurance, image assessment, full supply-chain custody, advanced ML/satellite/IoT and subscription billing are outside MVP. Baseline ownership/security/recovery is never deferred to Phase 24.

Net income with overhead/depreciation/finance costs is a source “later” item with timing undecided; do not label gross margin net profit. [Financial integrity](../architecture/financial-integrity.md), [non-goals](non-goals.md) and [roadmap](../MASTER-IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md) define limits.
