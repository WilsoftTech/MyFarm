These are **research hypotheses**, not validated customer profiles. Source P0031–P0057 names segments but contains no interviews, literacy distribution or smartphone statistics.

| Persona | Job/problem hypothesis | Primary workflow | Validation needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poultry farmer | Frequent feed, mortality and egg/sales records are hard to reconcile | Batch → expense/production → closing flock/margin | Actual record frequency, batch units, phone access and trust in calculations |
| Crop farmer | Costs and harvests scattered across plots/seasons | Plot → season → inputs/labour → harvest/sale | Seasonal calendars, area units, losses, perennial crops |
| Farm manager | Records work for an owner and coordinates workers | Scoped membership → activities → records/reports | Who can approve corrections, delegation and shared phones |
| Extension officer | Helps farmers record/act without independent app operation | Assigned farmer → visit/photos → recommendation/follow-up | Consent/assignment authority, offline device and organization responsibility |

Do not assume farmers have email, exclusive device access, English literacy or reliable charging/network. Recruitment should include variation in device/network and recordkeeping practices, with sampling decisions approved under Q01/Q02.
