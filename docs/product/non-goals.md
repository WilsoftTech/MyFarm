Initial MVP will not deliver a universal agricultural ecosystem, consumer e-commerce, AI diagnoses, autonomous financial writes, custody/payment processing, credit scoring, lending/insurance underwriting, satellite/IoT infrastructure or commercial billing. Future roadmap entries remain planned, not removed.

MyFarm initially connects farmers to financial partners rather than acting as lender. Farm Economic Profile is not a universal credit score. Traceability is not proof of certification/export compliance. Forecasts/model confidence are not guarantees. Poultry/crops are initial workflows; enum values for dairy/fish/other do not promise full modules.

Avoid microservices, Kubernetes, event-bus infrastructure and vector databases until measured requirements justify them. A modular Next.js application and managed PostgreSQL fit the requested solo-developer baseline.

Current authorization is Phase 0 research/preparation and Markdown status updates only. No application features, production migrations, infrastructure, paid subscriptions or production-data changes.
