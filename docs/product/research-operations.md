## Draft participant consent script

Explain in a language the participant understands:

“We are learning how farmers keep records, manage farm work and understand costs and income to help design MyFarm. We would like to ask about your current practices and, if you agree, watch you demonstrate how you record a recent farm event. Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Participation does not promise payment, credit, insurance, better yields or access to a future service.

We will use a participant code instead of your name in shared research summaries. Before we begin, the interviewer will explain who may see the notes, where they are kept, how long they are kept and how to contact the research lead about your information. We will not request national identity numbers, bank credentials, passwords or copies of financial accounts.”

The interviewer must provide actual research-lead contact, note location/access and retention date before use; these are not approved yet. This is a research script, not a claim of legal compliance.

Separate choices:
- Participation and written anonymized notes: yes/no.
- Demonstration of current records, without taking copies: yes/no.
- Optional quotation in de-identified synthesis: yes/no.
- Optional recording/photo: yes/no, with separate purpose/retention explanation. Default is no recording/photo.

Record participant code, script version P00-CONSENT-DRAFT-1, explanation language, choices, interviewer and timestamp in the restricted consent log. Silence is not consent. Declined participation ends the interview; do not retain unnecessary notes. Never publish names, phone numbers, precise location or raw sensitive records in docs/.

Observation exercises, only with consent:
1. Ask the farmer to demonstrate a recent expense/income record using their current method; do not present a nonexistent app.
2. Walk through one crop cycle or poultry batch: opening inputs/birds, costs, work, production, sales, losses and closing stock.
3. Ask what would make a useful weekly return to recordkeeping. Record the response as stated intent, not proven retention.
4. Ask which wording is understandable; compare neutral expense phrasing without leading to desired agreement.
